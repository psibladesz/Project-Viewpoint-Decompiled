The screen-space reflections' checks of a hit (shaders/reflection_trace.frag, D42) follow AMD's FidelityFX
Stochastic Screen Space Reflections (https://github.com/GPUOpen-Effects/FidelityFX-SSSR, ffx-sssr/ffx_sssr.h), as
Godot's rework takes them: a hit off the screen, on the sky, on a face turned away or behind the depth it met by
more than a tolerance is not one. FidelityFX SSSR is licensed under the MIT License, which follows.
