LWJGL's Vulkan bindings (org.lwjgl.vulkan, lwjgl-vulkan 3.4.1, https://github.com/LWJGL/lwjgl3) are carried in
Viewpoint's jar for the upscalers' Vulkan device (upscale.VulkanDevice, D48), unchanged; the game ships the rest of
LWJGL. The vendors' libraries the upscalers call ship in the mod's 42/natives folder, each beside its own licence
(fidelityfx-sdk-license.txt, xess-license.txt, streamline-license.txt, nvngx_dlss.license.txt). LWJGL is licensed under
